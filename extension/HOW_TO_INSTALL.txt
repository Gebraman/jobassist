Welcome to JobAssist AI Studio! Follow these 3 simple steps to load your extension
and activate your administrator-approved candidate license.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: UNPACK THE DOWNLOADED ZIP FOLDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Right-click the downloaded "JobAssist-AI-Studio-Extension.zip" file.
2. Select "Extract All..." and extract it into a folder on your computer
   (e.g., Desktop or Documents).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: LOAD INTO GOOGLE CHROME (DEVELOPER MODE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Google Chrome.
2. In the URL address bar, type:
   chrome://extensions
   and press Enter.
3. In the top-right corner, toggle the switch for "Developer mode" to ON.
4. Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top-left corner.
5. Select the unzipped folder containing this extension!
6. Click the Extensions (puzzle piece) icon in your Chrome toolbar and PIN
   "JobAssist AI Studio" for instant 1-click access.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: ACTIVATE YOUR ADMINISTRATOR LICENSE KEY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⚠️ IMPORTANT:
To protect candidate privacy and system integrity, JobAssist features are
LOCKED until activated with an approved License Key.

1. Click the JobAssist extension icon to open the Studio Sidepanel.
2. If this is your first time, the Studio will prompt: "Enter License Key".
3. Enter the License Key issued to you by the platform administrator
   (e.g., PREM-XXXX-XXXX).
4. Click "Unlock Studio".
5. Your pass (Daily, 3-Day Flex, Monthly Pro, or Yearly VIP) is now active!
